# Questions for Organizers

## Competition format

- Does Week 1 count toward final scoring?
- Does scoring reset after the top-100 checkpoint?

## Trading rules

- What is the exact Sharpe ratio formula?
- What is the data frequency?
- What leverage is allowed?
- What are the liquidation rules?
- Are API, MT5, and chat interface all separate trading options?

## Market simulator

- Is the simulated market purely synthetic?
- Is it connected to real market events?
- Can the simulator change during the competition?
